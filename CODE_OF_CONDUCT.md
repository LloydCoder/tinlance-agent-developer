# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in TADL a respectful and professional experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assuming good faith while addressing disagreements directly and professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, sexualized conduct or unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, abuse, or retaliation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take corrective action that is appropriate and proportionate.

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately to **@LloydCoder** through GitHub. Do not create a public issue for sensitive conduct reports.

Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in good faith. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
